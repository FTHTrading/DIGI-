# START-TO-FINISH FUNDING WORKFLOW

**Dignity Institutional Platform — Funding Operations Reference**

> The platform organizes evidence, documents, approvals, and funding workflows. It does not itself act as a broker-dealer, bank, custodian, legal adviser, investment adviser, or source of fabricated proof of funds. Any regulated activity must be handled by qualified counsel and properly licensed entities.

---

## The Core Mental Model

```
Asset → Evidence → Packet → Review Room → Term Sheet → Closing → Controlled Funding → Reporting → Exit
```

Lead with:
- real asset
- real owner
- real documents
- real valuation
- real compliance path
- real capital source
- real closing process
- real reporting

Blockchain and Systec L1 come in **after** the funding workflow is legitimate — as the settlement, audit, reserve, and transfer-control layer.

## Stage 2 — Entity and Sponsor Verification

Before any funding conversation begins, the system verifies who owns what.

**Verification checklist:**
- KYC / KYB
- Beneficial ownership
- Corporate documents
- Operating agreements
- Good standing certificates
- Board approvals
- Signing authority
- Sanctions / OFAC screening
- Accredited investor / qualified buyer status where applicable

**Compliance note:** For securities-style offerings, Regulation D and Rule 506 are common private-offering paths, but the exact structure requires securities counsel. SEC guidance states that Rule 506(b) offerings can raise unlimited amounts from accredited investors, while Rule 506(c) generally requires accredited-investor verification when general solicitation is used.

> Exact exemption, filing, licensing, and offering requirements must be confirmed by qualified counsel and properly licensed partners.

## Stage 3 — Asset Evidence File

This is where Dignity becomes useful. The system builds a structured evidence file.

**Evidence components:**
- Ownership proof
- Valuation
- Reserve report
- Custodian letter
- Engineering report
- Geology report
- Appraisal
- Contracts
- Revenue records
- Title documents
- Insurance
- Permits
- Photos
- Audit trail

**Correct proof language — do not use fake "proof of funds" claims:**

| Term | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Proof of Asset (POA) | Verified ownership evidence for the underlying asset |
| Proof of Control (POC) | Verified authority to encumber, transfer, or pledge |
| Proof of Reserve (POR) | Custodian or registry attestation of held asset |
| Proof of Valuation | Appraisal, reserve report, or mark-to-market evidence |
| Proof of Source | Verified origin of the asset and funds used to acquire it |
| Proof of Use of Funds | Documented draw schedule, invoices, and disbursement plan |
| Proof of Closing Path | Verified legal structure, counsel engagement, and timeline |

Use POF (Proof of Funds) **only** when there is actual verified money in a verified account.

## Stage 5 — Capital Structure Selection

The system does not assume every deal requires tokenization. Possible structures:

| Structure Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Senior debt | Traditional secured loan against asset |
| Bridge loan | Short-term financing pending longer-term close |
| Private credit | Non-bank lending with negotiated terms |
| Equity investment | Direct ownership stake |
| Preferred equity | Priority return with equity participation |
| Revenue share | Fixed percentage of future revenues |
| Offtake agreement | Forward purchase of asset output |
| Forward purchase | Committed acquisition at a future date |
| SPV interest | Interest in a purpose-built special purpose vehicle |
| Asset-backed note | Debt instrument secured by identified asset |
| Reserve-backed tokenized security | On-chain instrument after full funding workflow completion |

**Blockchain / RWA rails come after:**
1. Asset is real and verified
2. Owner is verified
3. Legal structure is selected
4. Capital source is identified
5. Compliance path is approved

## Stage 6 — Investor / Lender Review

The platform routes the packet to appropriate capital sources:

- Private lenders
- Family offices
- Funds
- Broker-dealer partners
- Commodity finance groups
- Real estate capital groups
- Project finance desks
- Strategic buyers
- Offtake partners

**Review room tracking:**
- Who reviewed the packet
- What documents were requested
- What questions came back
- Whether they declined
- Whether they issued an LOI
- Whether they issued a term sheet

**Compliance note:** For larger institutional resale or private-market transactions, Rule 144A is generally tied to qualified institutional buyers (QIBs). Rule 144A describes a private resale rule for institutions and defines QIB status as generally requiring at least $100M in AUM in securities of non-affiliates.

---

## Stage 7 — Term Sheet / LOI

Once interest exists, the system creates or tracks:

**Term sheet components:**
- LOI / term sheet document
- Funding amount
- Interest rate / return target
- Collateral
- Equity percentage
- Fees
- Closing conditions
- Due diligence items
- Escrow requirements
- Legal documents required
- Timeline

---

## Stage 8 — Compliance and Legal Closing

Before money moves:

**Closing checklist:**
- Attorney review
- Securities exemption review (if applicable)
- Lender documents
- Subscription agreement (if applicable)
- Purchase agreement
- Escrow agreement
- Custody agreement
- Board approval
- Investor accreditation / QIB verification where required
- AML / KYC clearance

**Compliance note:** If an issuer uses a Regulation D exemption, SEC guidance states that Form D is used to file notice of an exempt securities offering, and is generally required within 15 days after the first sale for Regulation D offerings.

---

## Stage 9 — Funding Close

Funds must not go directly into uncontrolled wallets or accounts.

**Professional close flow:**
1. Investor/lender signs documents
2. Funds sent to escrow, qualified account, or custodian
3. Closing checklist completed
4. Board or authorized signer approves
5. Funds released per use-of-funds schedule
6. Audit event recorded

---

## Stage 12 — Repayment / Redemption / Exit

**Platform tracks:**
- Scheduled repayment
- Refinance
- Asset sale
- Redemption
- Buyback
- Secondary transfer (where legally allowed)

---

## Compliance Boundary

The platform organizes evidence, documents, approvals, and funding workflows. It does not itself act as a broker-dealer, bank, custodian, legal adviser, investment adviser, or source of fabricated proof of funds. Any regulated activity must be handled by qualified counsel and properly licensed entities.

Systec custodies and attests. Dignity governs and enforces. No autonomous live execution without explicit board approval gates.
