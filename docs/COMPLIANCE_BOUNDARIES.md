# Compliance Boundaries

This platform is an operational software system. It is not itself a regulated financial institution.

## Explicit Non-Claims

- The platform is not a broker-dealer by itself.
- The platform is not a bank.
- The platform is not a custodian.
- The platform is not a legal or financial adviser.
- The platform does not create fake proof of funds.
- The platform does not bypass securities, banking, lending, or AML/KYC requirements.

## What the Platform Does

- Organizes workflows, evidence, documents, approvals, and operational records
- Tracks readiness state for onboarding, compliance, and funding packet preparation
- Supports auditable review and approval trails
- Provides structured outputs for professional review

## Regulated Activity Boundary

Any regulated activity must be handled by properly licensed professionals/entities, including (as applicable):

- Licensed broker-dealers
- Licensed custodians
- Regulated banking/payment institutions
- Qualified legal counsel and compliance professionals
- Authorized operators with explicit approval authority

## Operational Policy

- No autonomous live financial execution without explicit approval gates
- No representation of licensing status unless verified and documented
- No suppression of legal/compliance review in critical workflows
