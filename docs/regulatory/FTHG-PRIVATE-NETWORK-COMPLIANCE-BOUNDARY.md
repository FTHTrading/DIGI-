# FTHG Private Network Compliance Boundary

## Policy statement
FTHG is a private-network gold reserve accounting and settlement instrument.

## Explicit constraints
- Not enabled as public payment stablecoin mode by default.
- External/public issuance requires regulated issuer or approved partner structure.
- All issuance-like flows must pass legal and compliance gates.

## Layer model
- Internal FTHG ledger: reserve accounting and settlement logic.
- Wrapped gold gateway: approved collateral intake.
- Reserve registry: proof of backing and attestations.
- Issuer compliance layer: legal pathway controls.
- Public payment stablecoin mode: disabled until approved.
