# BSA AML and Sanctions for Stablecoins

## Program components
- KYC and KYB onboarding
- OFAC sanctions screening
- PEP screening
- Ongoing transaction monitoring
- Suspicious activity workflow and escalation
- Records retention and exam readiness

## Operational workflows
- Risk scoring and customer tiering
- Alert triage and case management
- Investigator approval chain
- Exception handling with full audit trail

## Evidence requirements
- Timestamped screening outcomes
- Rule version capture
- Decision provenance logs
