# Bank Partner Stablecoin Gateway

## Objective
Enable regulated bank/trust counterparties to connect to FTH infrastructure without transferring core governance control.

## Integration model
- Partner onboarding profile
- Compliance and legal policy mapping
- Reserve and reporting data feed contracts
- Transaction policy guardrails
- Controlled settlement integration

## Partner controls
- Role-based approvals
- Regulatory report exports
- Audit-ready event logs
- Incident and exception escalation
