# OCC GENIUS Act Stablecoin Breakdown

## Why this matters
Stablecoins are being treated as regulated payment infrastructure, not an ungoverned crypto product category.

## Core implications for platform design
- Build as infrastructure-first: gateway, compliance, proof, reserves, settlement.
- Avoid public issuer claims unless operating through approved regulatory structure.
- Preserve auditable, board-governed, evidence-backed workflows.
