# Permitted Issuer Partner Model

## Goal
Allow FTH to operate as infrastructure while approved issuers handle regulated issuance activities.

## Responsibility split
- Partner issuer: issuance authority, issuer regulatory obligations.
- FTH platform: reserve proof, workflow, compliance tooling, audit evidence, settlement orchestration.

## Required controls
- Issuer partner approval workflow
- Jurisdiction and eligibility checks
- Monthly report and certification support
- Revocation and offboarding process

## Checklists
- PPSI readiness checklist
- Reserve certification checklist
- AML/BSA checklist
- Sanctions checklist
- Monthly reporting checklist
- Executive certification workflow checklist
