# PPSI Operating Model

## Operating posture
FTH/Dignity operates as institutional infrastructure that can support:
- Federally regulated issuer models
- State-qualified issuer models
- Bank/trust partner issuer models
- Non-issuer infrastructure provider models

## Functional control planes
- Issuer governance and approvals
- Reserve and liquidity controls
- Compliance monitoring and case management
- Settlement and reconciliation controls
- Audit evidence and reporting controls

## Boundary
Public payment stablecoin issuance mode remains disabled unless approved legal and regulatory pathway is active.
